# Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)

## Overview & Benefit Cover
- **Coverage Amount**: Up to ₹5 Lakh (5,00,000 INR) per eligible family per year for secondary and tertiary healthcare hospitalization.
- **Cashless & Paperless**: Free treatment, medicines, diagnostic tests, and food during hospitalization at all empanelled public and private hospitals across India.
- **Pre-existing Conditions**: All pre-existing medical conditions are covered from day one.
- **Family Cap**: No cap on family size, age, or gender.

## Eligibility Criteria
- Based on Socio-Economic Caste Census 2011 (SECC 2011) rural and urban deprivation criteria.
- Rural: Households living in one-room kutcha houses, female-headed households with no adult male, landless manual labour households, SC/ST households.
- Urban: Designated occupational categories of workers (street vendors, rag pickers, domestic workers, construction workers, sanitation workers, transport workers).
- Active Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) and Priority Household (PHH) ration card holders in most participating states.

## How to Access & Verify Eligibility
- **Official Toll-Free Helpline**: 14555 or 1800-111-565.
- **Verification Portal**: mera.pmjay.gov.in or visit the nearest Primary Health Centre (PHC), Community Health Centre (CHC), or Ayushman Mitra kiosk at any empanelled district hospital.
- **Required Documents**: Aadhaar card or Ration card.
