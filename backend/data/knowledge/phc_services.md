# Primary Health Centre (PHC) Standard Services

## Clinical Care & OPD Timings
- **OPD Timings**: Usually 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM (Monday to Saturday, emergency services 24x7 at 24/7 designated PHCs).
- **Outpatient Care**: Routine medical consultations for fever, respiratory illnesses, diarrhea, skin infections, minor injuries, and chronic illness follow-ups.

## Free Essential Diagnostics at PHC
- Rapid diagnostic tests for Malaria, Dengue, and Chikungunya.
- Haemoglobin (Hb) test for anaemia screening.
- Blood sugar test (fasting & random) for diabetes.
- Urine routine examination (protein, sugar, pregnancy test).
- Sputum collection and testing for Tuberculosis (TB / Nikshay programme).

## Maternal & Child Health (MCH)
- Antenatal care (ANC) checkups including blood pressure, weight, IFA (iron-folic acid) tablets, calcium supplementation.
- Village Health and Nutrition Days (VHND) sessions conducted with ASHA and ANM workers.
- Growth monitoring and malnutrition screening for under-5 children.

## Essential Medicines Dispensary
- Free essential medicines including antipyretics, antibiotics, ORS (Oral Rehydration Salts), Zinc tablets, iron syrups, and hypertensive medications supplied free under national health missions.
