# Universal Immunization Programme (UIP) - National Immunization Schedule

## At Birth
- **BCG**: Against Tuberculosis (single dose, intradermal, left upper arm).
- **OPV-0**: Oral Polio Vaccine zero dose (oral drops).
- **Hepatitis B birth dose**: Within 24 hours of birth (intramuscular, anterolateral thigh).

## At 6 Weeks, 10 Weeks, and 14 Weeks
- **Pentavalent Vaccine (1, 2, 3)**: Protects against 5 diseases: Diphtheria, Pertussis (whooping cough), Tetanus, Hepatitis B, and Haemophilus influenzae type b (Hib).
- **Oral Polio Vaccine (OPV 1, 2, 3)**: Oral polio drops.
- **Rotavirus Vaccine (RVV 1, 2, 3)**: Oral drops preventing severe childhood diarrhoea.
- **Inactivated Polio Vaccine (fIPV 1 & 2)**: Fractional dose intradermal given at 6 and 14 weeks.
- **Pneumococcal Conjugate Vaccine (PCV 1 & 2)**: Protects against pneumococcal pneumonia (given at 6 and 14 weeks).

## At 9 to 12 Months
- **MR 1st Dose**: Measles and Rubella combination vaccine.
- **PCV Booster**: Pneumococcal Conjugate Vaccine booster dose.
- **JE 1st Dose**: Japanese Encephalitis (in endemic districts).
- **Vitamin A (1st dose)**: 1 ml oral solution for eye health and immune boost.

## At 16 to 24 Months
- **MR 2nd Dose**: Measles and Rubella 2nd dose.
- **DPT Booster-1**: Diphtheria, Pertussis, Tetanus booster.
- **OPV Booster**: Oral polio booster dose.
- **JE 2nd Dose**: Japanese Encephalitis 2nd dose (endemic areas).
- **Vitamin A (Doses 2 to 9)**: Administered every 6 months until child turns 5 years old.

## School Age & Adolescents
- **5 to 6 Years**: DPT Booster-2.
- **10 Years & 16 Years**: Td (Tetanus & adult Diphtheria) vaccine.
