# Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram (JSSK)

## Benefits for Pregnant Women
- **100% Free Institutional Delivery**: Completely free normal delivery and Caesarean section (C-section) in all public health institutions (PHCs, CHCs, District Hospitals).
- **Free Medicines & Consumables**: All necessary drugs, surgical supplies, and IV fluids provided at zero cost during hospital stay.
- **Free Diagnostics**: Free blood tests, urine tests, ultrasound, and monitoring during pregnancy and delivery.
- **Free Diet**: Nutritious food provided free of charge for 3 days (normal delivery) or 7 days (C-section).
- **Free Referral Transport**: Free ambulance transport from home to hospital, inter-facility transfer in case of complications, and drop back home after discharge (dial 108 or 102).

## Benefits for Sick Newborns and Infants (Up to 1 Year)
- Free inpatient and outpatient medical treatment in all government health facilities.
- Free essential drugs and consumables.
- Free diagnostics (pathology and radiology).
- Free blood and blood component transfusion.
- Free emergency transport between home and hospital.
